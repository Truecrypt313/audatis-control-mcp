# Commercial licensing for audatisControl

audatisControl is published under a **dual-licensing model**:

- **Noncommercial use:** Available under the [PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0](LICENSE.md).
- **Commercial use:** Requires a separate **written commercial license agreement**, negotiated with the rights holder **before** the commercial use begins.

The noncommercial license is the governing text for uses it permits. This page explains the separate commercial licensing route; it does **not** narrow or modify rights already granted by PolyForm Noncommercial 1.0.0.

## When to request a commercial license

If you want to use audatisControl in a commercial context, such as a paid service, a customer project, commercial consulting, or an internal business deployment not covered by the noncommercial license, request commercial terms first. Whether a particular use is noncommercial depends on the license terms and facts; do not assume that use without direct revenue is automatically noncommercial.

Commercial licenses can cover internal deployment, hosting, redistribution, support, or other agreed rights. Scope, pricing, duration, support, and other conditions are **negotiated individually**. No commercial license or payment amount is granted or set automatically by this document.
